Zomato Product Teardown 

Overview
A product teardown of Zomato, exploring its user experience, product opportunities, and potential improvements to its food ordering experience.

Project Objective
To understand the user problem, analyse the existing product experience, and identify opportunities to improve the user journey through product thinking.

Problem Statement
Planning food for gatherings and occasions can be challenging due to uncertainty around quantities, budget, food preferences, and choosing the right combination of dishes.

Proposed Opportunity: Occasion Planner
An experience that helps users plan food orders for gatherings based on their requirements.

Key considerations
- Number of guests
- Budget
- Occasion
- Cuisine preferences
- Dietary restrictions
- Meal type

Product Opportunities
- Automatic food quantity estimation
- Budget-friendly alternatives
- Ability to manually edit quantities
- Personalised recommendations based on preferences

Success Metrics
- Quantity satisfaction
- Reduction in excess food
- Reduction in insufficient food
- Feature adoption and conversion
